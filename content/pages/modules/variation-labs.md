## Why

While the four sessions build, you're not waiting: you're directing. Steer one with a design note, queue a question for when another is done, and keep an eye on all of them from the Sessions list. When they're done, the lab puts every direction in one place, so you can compare them on the real app with a keystroke.

## On Excalidraw

**Do this right after the four sessions start.** They can finish in about 3 minutes, so steer and queue while they're still working.

**Steer a running session.** In the Sessions list, open one of the four. Type this, then open the menu on the **Send** button and choose **Steer with Message**. It reaches the session after its next step:

```text label="Prompt 5a, steer"
Give every button a tooltip with its name and keyboard shortcut.
```

**Queue a message.** Open a different one of the four. Type this, open the **Send** menu and choose **Add to Queue**. It waits until that session is done:

```text label="Prompt 5b, queue"
When you're done, tell me in three bullets what makes this toolbar different.
```

**Already finished?** If a session is done before you get to it, just click **Send**. Your message goes in as its next step.

**Add your own idea.** Watch a direction as it comes together, and steer it with one design note of your own.

**Tour the Sessions list while they build:**

- The four sessions sit nested under the orchestrator. Collapse it to tuck them away.
- Each one shows whether it's working, waiting on you or done.
- Pin the ones you care about, and archive what you're finished with.

**Open the lab.** When the orchestrator says all four are done, open its lab link in the integrated browser. Press <kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>]</kbd> to switch toolbars, or use the switcher. Pick your favorite.

## You should see

Each toolbar live on the real app, one at a time, with the same drawing underneath. The steered session's buttons have tooltips, and the queued question gets its three bullets when that session finishes.

## If it doesn't

- **A session is stuck.** Open it and say: "What's left? Finish the smallest version that works."
- **No lab link.** Say: "Give me the lab link and open it in the integrated browser."
- **The switcher doesn't show.** Each toolbar also has its own address: add `?toolbar=<name>` to the localhost link.

## Steering or queuing {collapsed}

- **Steer** reaches a running session after its next step. Use it to change direction mid-flight.
- **Queue** waits until the current work is done. Use it for a follow-up, like asking for a summary.
- **Stop and Send** stops what it's doing and starts on your message.
- **Ask in Side Chat** asks a question without interrupting it.

## On your own work

The same steer and queue work in any session. A good steer is short and specific, like a design review comment: "Use our spacing scale", "Make the empty state friendlier", "Check it at phone width".
