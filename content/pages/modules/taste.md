## Why

You've given agents plenty of feedback already: every "not like that", every "make it calmer", every time you fixed spacing yourself. Pull it into one skill, **my-taste**, and every prototype from now on starts closer to what you'd make. It's built from your own sessions and notes, on your laptop, and it stays private.

You installed my-taste in setup if you checked it. If you didn't, paste the [setup prompt](setup.html#setup-prompt) again in a new chat and check it in the list.

## On Excalidraw

Tonight's sessions are a good first source: your marked-up screenshot, your steers and the favorite you picked. In a new chat:

```text label="Prompt 11.1"
Use the my-taste skill to learn from tonight's Excalidraw sessions: my marked-up screenshot, the steers I sent, the toolbar I picked and why. Add what you learn to my-taste as short rules, each with where it came from. Show me the rules before you save them.
```

## You should see

A short list of rules, like "prefers fewer, larger targets" or "labels over icons when there's room", each pointing at the moment it came from. Nothing is saved until you say so.

## On your own work

The more of your real feedback it reads, the better it gets. Point it at notes you keep, design critiques you've written, and your sessions:

```text label="Prompt 11.2"
Build my taste with the my-taste skill. Read my sessions here from the last few weeks, where I corrected an agent or asked for something different, and <a folder of my design notes or critiques>. Merge what you find into my-taste as rules, each with where it came from and when. Keep it on this laptop: nothing gets published.
```

It never reads your email or chat apps. Only what you point it at.

## How it's used

Skills load when they fit the work. Ask for a prototype or a variation, and my-taste comes along, so the first version already follows your rules. To keep it current on its own, set up a weekly update in [Module {n:self-improvement}](module:self-improvement).
