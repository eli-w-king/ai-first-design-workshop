## Why

Thanks for spending your evening with us. Pick one thing to hand off this week, and see what you get back.

## Before you go

- **Set the reminder.** The free month renews on its own. Cancel tomorrow or next week: [here's how](after.html#cancel-the-free-month).
- **Keep what you made.** Your Excalidraw copy, its pull request and your live link are yours.
- **Share the link.** Your page for everyone is a nice thing to show someone.

## Where things live

- [Cheat sheets](cheat-sheets.html): the terms and controls that come up most, on one page.
- [FAQ](faq.html): the questions we expect most.
- [After the course](after.html): how to keep going, and how to cancel.
- [Uninstall](uninstall.html): how to take it all back out, with a video.
- Every module page stays up, so you can come back to any of them.
