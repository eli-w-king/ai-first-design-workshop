## Do I need to know how to code?

No. You'll describe what you want in plain words, look at the result and give feedback, like a design review. We'll explain the few engineering words that come up as we go, and the [cheat sheets](cheat-sheets.html) have them all.

## Do I need a Mac?

No. Bring a Mac or a Windows laptop. A personal laptop works best, since work laptops often block installs.

## Is there anything to do before I come?

No prework. If you can, install [VS Code Insiders](https://code.visualstudio.com/insiders/) and make a [GitHub account](https://github.com/signup). We do everything else together, in the room.

## Does it cost anything?

The workshop is free. You get a free month of GitHub Copilot Max: you add a payment method when you redeem, and it renews on its own, so set a reminder to [cancel](after.html#cancel-the-free-month) tomorrow or next week. If you already have Copilot, use the plan you have.

## What gets installed on my laptop?

A folder in your home folder, `ai-first-workshop`, with your copy of Excalidraw, the workshop skills and resources, plus Git, Node.js and the GitHub CLI if you don't have them. The setup checklist shows every item and its size before you confirm. The [uninstall guide](uninstall.html) takes it all back out.

## Does anything get sent or posted for me?

No messages, posts or purchases. Your work goes to your own GitHub repo: a pull request you can see, merged when you ask, and a public GitHub Pages link for your copy of Excalidraw.

## Is my Excalidraw copy public?

Yes. Your copy and its live link are public on GitHub, like Excalidraw itself. Don't put anything private in it.

## Is this connected to the Excalidraw team?

No. Excalidraw is an open-source whiteboard under the MIT license. We play the team that owns it, with fictional practice material, and nothing goes to their repository.

## I fell behind. Now what?

Raise your hand. Every module page has the prompt for each step and what to do if it doesn't work, so you can catch up at your own pace, tonight or later.

## Is this an official Microsoft or GitHub event?

No. It's an independent community event with UXPA Seattle.

## Where is it?

In person in Seattle. The address is in your event registration.
