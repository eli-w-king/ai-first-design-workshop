Everything you made stays yours. Here's how to keep going, and how to make sure the free month doesn't turn into a bill.

## Cancel the free month

The free month of GitHub Copilot Max renews on its own. If you don't want to pay for the next month, cancel before it renews. You keep everything until the end of the month you have.

1. On GitHub, click your profile picture, then **Settings**.
2. Open **Billing and licensing**, then **Licensing** ([go there directly](https://github.com/settings/billing/licensing)).
3. In the GitHub Copilot section, choose **Manage subscription**, then **Cancel subscription**.

If you had Copilot before the workshop, keep the plan you had. Extra usage is billed separately, so a charge can still show up after you cancel. Setting a $0 budget, [as in setup](setup.html#redeem), stops that. ([GitHub's docs](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot/how-tos/manage-your-account/view-and-change-your-copilot-plan))
