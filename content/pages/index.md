A hands-on evening for designers. We'll set up VS Code Insiders together, then take one real open-source app, Excalidraw, from a marked-up screenshot to four directions built in parallel, a demo video and a live link.

## What you'll make

<ol class="makes" role="list">
<li>A redesign of Excalidraw's toolbar, from your own marked-up screenshot.</li>
<li>Four very different directions, built at the same time by agents.</li>
<li>A pull request, a narrated demo video and your own live link.</li>
</ol>

## Bring

- Your own Mac or Windows laptop, and its charger. A personal laptop works best, since work laptops often block installs.
- A GitHub account. If you don't have one yet, make one at [github.com](https://github.com/signup).
- If you can, install [VS Code Insiders](https://code.visualstudio.com/insiders/) before you come. Everything else happens in the room, together.

The address is in your event registration.

## After tonight

The whole course stays here. The [use case](module:open-on-localhost) has every prompt from the evening, and [On your own time](module:taste) covers what we won't get to: your taste, automations, research, overnight runs and working from another computer.
