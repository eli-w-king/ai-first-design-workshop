Skola 2.0 in one evening

Your fork of Skola, not an official release. The goal is a tested fix, a redesign from your own markup, Listen mode, three directions, a narrated demo and a working site beside your repo.

01

Fix it and prove it

Make Add Card save, with a test that fails before and passes after.

02

Redesign from your markup

Use arrows, circles, notes and crop to change the real study screen.

03

Add Listen mode

Hear the visible card on demand, while keeping studying usable in silence.

04

Try three directions

Compare different Today homes that help you choose what to study.

05

Make a narrated demo

Record a short real flow while another independent task runs.

06

Share your fork

Leave with a live link and a repo link, crediting Skola and naming your own changes.
