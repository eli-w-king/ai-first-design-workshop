# 03 | High school teacher

The teacher brought a spreadsheet with a vocabulary term in the first column and an example in the second. They use it for the first five minutes of class. Starting with an empty deck, they copied one row by hand and stopped at the second. "There are twenty more rows and the bell's about to go." Once shown the file import, they flipped two cards to make sure the examples hadn't ended up on the front.

At the projector, they let the class discuss a card before showing the answer. They wouldn't want the app to pronounce a word automatically. That might be a useful clue on Tuesday and give away the exercise on Wednesday. They also covered the end-screen percentage with the projector remote. Different students had answered different cards, so it wasn't anybody's score.

There was a twist when we asked what a good home screen would highlight. The class deck wasn't always the one with the most cards due. They wanted a quick way back to the deck they'd just prepared for that period. "My next class isn't whichever pile is biggest."
