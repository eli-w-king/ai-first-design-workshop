# 05 | Commuter learning Spanish

This learner reviews phrases on the bus. They picked a card with *¿Me avisa en mi parada?* on the front, waited until the bus was quiet, and tried saying it. They could recognize the phrase but weren't sure where to put the stress. "I can read the whole thing. I want to hear where your voice goes." Hearing the Spanish was the first change they asked for. A generated English explanation wasn't what was missing.

They reached for an earbud, then put it back when their stop was called. On the next card they wanted to read in silence, not leave the session to find a sound setting. If a voice isn't available on their phone, they'd rather keep studying than wait for a download. They practice on this route partly because it works when reception is poor.

When asked how many cards were left, they checked the bus display before checking the app. "I don't need to finish the deck. I need to get off the bus." Knowing they'd done three cards would feel useful even if they had to stop with seven left.
