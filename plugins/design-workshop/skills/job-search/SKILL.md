# Job search

Help with the parts of a job search that take the most time, while the person stays in charge of every word that
goes out.

## Rules

1. **Drafts only.** Never apply, send, post or message anyone. The person reviews and sends everything themselves.
2. **Only what they did.** Use their resume, case studies and notes. Never invent a project, a number, a title or a
   result. If something would help but isn't in their material, ask.
3. **Paste, don't scrape.** Work from postings the person pastes or links to on a company's own site. Don't log in
   to job sites or scrape them.
4. **Their voice.** If they have a `my-voice` skill, follow it.
5. **Keep it private.** Save drafts in the current folder, never in a public repo.

## Tailor to a posting

1. Read the posting and the person's resume and case studies.
2. Draft a 4 line intro and 3 resume bullets for this role, each tied to something they actually did.
3. List which case study fits best, and one sentence on why.
4. Show the draft, and say which parts of the posting each line answers.

## Interview prep

1. Read the company's public website and product pages.
2. List 8 likely questions for the role. For each one, the story from their case studies that answers it best, in
   two lines.
3. Offer a mock interview: you ask, they answer, and you give one specific note per answer.

## A weekly roles digest

Offer the automation template "Weekly roles digest" from this plugin. It only drafts a file. The person picks the
role, the place, and the sites, and reviews the list each week.
