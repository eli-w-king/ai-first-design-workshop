# Voice and taste

Teach the agent how this person writes and what they care about in design, from their own material. It stays on
their laptop.

## Rules

1. **Only the files the person points to.** Don't search their computer, their email, or their accounts.
2. **Keep it private.** Save to the person's own skills folder, never to a repo, and never publish it.
3. **Their words, not yours.** Rules come from what's in the files. Quote short examples word for word.

## Steps

1. Ask which files to read: for writing, case studies, portfolio text, a bio, posts or messages they wrote. For
   taste, design feedback they gave or got (pasted in), their case studies' "why" sections, designs they admire and
   why.
2. Read them, then draft two short files:
   - **My voice:** how they open and close, sentence length, words they use and avoid, tone, and three short real
     examples.
   - **My taste:** 8 to 15 design rules in their words, each with where it came from (the file and a short quote).
3. Show both drafts. Change anything they disagree with.
4. With their okay, save them as skills in their personal skills folder: `my-voice/SKILL.md` and
   `my-taste/SKILL.md`, each with a `name` and a `description` that says when to use it.

## Using them

When the person asks for writing or design review, read `my-voice` or `my-taste` first, and say which rules you
followed. When they correct you, offer to add the correction to the file, and only add it with their okay.
