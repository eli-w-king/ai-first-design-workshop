# Fictional practice notes

**These notes and quoted sentences are invented for synthesis practice. No interview took place. They are not customer evidence, and they are not the final workshop dataset.** They refer to no named product or real organization. Use a person's chosen notes instead when provided.

## N1: fictional participant P01

- Task: find an item they had saved earlier in an unnamed interface.
- Direction: A, shown first.
- Observation: paused at the opening screen, then explored a secondary menu.
Invented practice quote: "I expected the things I saved to be easier to find."

## N2: fictional participant P02

- Task: the same saved-item task.
- Direction: B, shown first.
- Observation: used a visible label and found the item.
Invented practice quote: "That label tells me where to start."

## N3: fictional participant P02

- Task: remove the saved item from the list.
- Direction: B.
- Observation: paused before using the removal control.
Invented practice quote: "I do not know whether this removes it from my list or deletes it."

## N4: fictional participant P03

- Task: find a saved item.
- Direction: A, shown after B.
- Observation: completed the task, then preferred the quieter opening screen.
Invented practice quote: "I do not need all my saved things on the first screen."
