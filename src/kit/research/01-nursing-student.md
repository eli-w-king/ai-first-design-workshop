# 01 | Nursing student

Between a lecture and a lab, this student had seven minutes and a paper list of anatomy terms. They chose the first one, wrote a question, and added the answer. Back in the deck, they couldn't find the card. They typed it a second time before looking for a way to check whether the first one had saved. "I don't want to learn the app twice just to make one card."

With a small deck already loaded, they went straight into review. When it was time to leave, they didn't look for a streak. They wanted to know which two terms to come back to before lab. A big percentage didn't help them remember either one. "Tell me what to look at while I'm walking down the hall."

They would import the list of terms, but not a whole deck of someone else's definitions. They turned their paper over and rewrote one definition from memory. That was the part they wanted to do themselves. Typing the same term into two different places was the part they wanted to skip.
