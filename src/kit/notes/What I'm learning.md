# What I'm learning

Practice notes for the AI-First Design Workshop. A running list of things I read or noticed this week, in my own words.
An automation turns new notes into flashcards each morning.

## Mon Nov 16
- Text contrast: WCAG AA asks for at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (18pt, or 14pt bold).
- Touch targets: Apple suggests at least 44 by 44 points. Material suggests 48 by 48 dp.
- Line length reads best somewhere around 50 to 75 characters per line.

## Tue Nov 17
- Recognition over recall: show options people can recognize instead of making them remember (one of Nielsen's 10 heuristics).
- Progressive disclosure: show the basics first and keep advanced options one step away.
- An empty state should say what goes here and give one clear first action.

## Wed Nov 18
- Doherty threshold: people stay engaged when a system responds within about 400 ms.
- Goal-gradient effect: people speed up as they get closer to a goal, so showing progress helps.
- Peak-end rule: people judge an experience mostly by its best (or worst) moment and how it ends. Make the last screen count.
- Spacing effect: reviewing over spread-out sessions beats cramming. That's the idea behind spaced repetition.
